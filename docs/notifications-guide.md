# Turning on Tranmere Tracker notifications

If notifications are off, you won't know when coaches message you, when a broadcast
goes out, or when you need to check in. It takes about 30 seconds.

> **iPhone + browser?** Apple does not allow notifications from a normal Safari tab. You **must** add
> Tranmere Tracker to your Home Screen and open it from that icon. This is the most common reason
> lads miss messages.

**The test:** once it's done, you'll see a green **✅ Notifications enabled** box on your
Home screen. If you don't see that box, it isn't switched on.

---

## iPhone

### Using the Tranmere Tracker app (App Store / TestFlight)
1. Update the app to the latest version.
2. Open it and log in. When iPhone asks **"Tranmere Tracker would like to send you notifications"**, tap **Allow**.
3. If you don't get asked, tap **🔔 Enable notifications** on Home.
4. If you tapped *Don't Allow* before: **Settings → Notifications → Tranmere Tracker →** turn on **Allow Notifications**.

### Using the website (Safari)
iPhones only allow website notifications once the site is added to your Home Screen.
1. Open **app.thesolarcampus.com** in **Safari** (not Chrome, Instagram or Snapchat's browser).
2. Tap the **Share** button (square with an arrow) → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open Tranmere Tracker **from the new Home Screen icon** and log in.
4. Tap **🔔 Enable notifications** → **Allow**.

## Android

### Using the Tranmere Tracker app (Play Store)
1. Update the app in the Play Store. If Home says *"Update Tranmere Tracker from the Play Store"*, you're on an old version.
2. Open it, tap **🔔 Enable notifications** → **Allow**.
3. If you tapped *Don't allow* before: **Settings → Apps → Tranmere Tracker → Notifications →** turn on.

### Using the website (Chrome)
1. Open **app.thesolarcampus.com** in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app** (recommended).
3. Tap **🔔 Enable notifications** → **Allow**.

## Still not getting them?
- **Focus / Do Not Disturb / Sleep mode** hides notifications. Check your Control Centre or quick settings.
- **Battery saver** (especially on Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei): **Settings → Apps → Tranmere Tracker → Battery → Unrestricted**.
- **New phone or reinstalled the app?** You have to switch notifications on again on each device.
- **Logged out?** Log back in and check for the green box.
- Still stuck? Show a member of staff your Home screen.

---

## For staff

- **Who isn't set up:** the staff dashboard shows a **🔔 Notifications switched on X/Y** card. Tap it to see exactly who won't get alerts, then chase them.
- **Staff need it too.** Safeguarding alerts, missed check-ins and the AM/PM attendance reports all come as notifications.
- **Every chat message and broadcast** sends a notification to everyone in that chat who has it switched on. You don't need to do anything extra.
- **Nudge button** in a chat re-sends an alert to members who haven't read it.
- **Quickest way to get everyone set up:** spend 5 minutes at the start of a session. Everyone opens the app, you check each phone for the green box. That works much better than asking them to do it at home.
